# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Municipal engineers, local disaster risk reduction officers (MDRRMO Luisiana), LGU urban planners, and local citizens of Luisiana, Laguna.
- **Engineers & Planners**: Review infrastructure project proposals, permit applications, zoning constraints, and site safety.
- **Disaster Response Officers**: Monitor live weather, rainfall thresholds, and AI geohazard / landslide / earthquake risk zones in real time.
- **Citizens**: Submit building applications, view infrastructure initiatives, and report hazards through an approachable, accessible public interface.

## Product Purpose
Deliver an intuitive, human-friendly GIS infrastructure management and hazard monitoring platform for the Municipality of Luisiana, Laguna. It replaces complex, opaque bureaucratic paperwork and disjointed GIS tools with clean, transparent, real-time spatial intelligence and simplified public workflows.

## Positioning
An accessible, high-utility municipal GIS platform that combines precision engineering review with human-friendly public safety tools—delivering critical spatial data without cognitive overload.

## Brand Personality
Human-friendly, clear, civic, and practical.
- Tone: Approachable yet reliable, plainspoken, and focused.
- Visual style: Modern flat design with intentional visual hierarchy, high legibility, and zero visual clutter.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, impenetrable legacy GIS interfaces drowning in cryptic toolbars and microscopic menus.
- Bureaucratic government portals with wall-of-text forms, confusing navigation, and poor error feedback.
- Faux-luxurious AI tropes: gratuitous glassmorphism, heavy drop shadows, side-stripe card borders (`border-left: 4px`), and low-contrast muted gray text.

## Design Principles
- **Human-friendly simplicity**: Design for clarity and ease of understanding across diverse user backgrounds, avoiding unnecessary technical jargon.
- **Flat design clarity**: Rely on crisp typographic hierarchy, clean solid surfaces, and purposeful spacing rather than heavy shadows or skeuomorphic bevels.
- **Map-first utility**: Keep the interactive 2D/3D map and spatial data central and unobstructed, with dockable, cleanly organized tool panels.
- **High legibility & tactical contrast**: Maintain strict WCAG contrast for all text and status cues (safe, warn, danger) across both light and dark operational modes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards for all body text, buttons, and form elements.
- Accessible color choices ensuring hazard indicators and status pills are distinguishable beyond color alone (using icons and descriptive text labels).
- Full keyboard operability for forms, modals, and interactive panels.
- Respect for reduced motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`) on all map transitions and UI animations.
